Reading the metrics

Accuracy — the share of all messages ScamSpotter judged correctly.

Precision — of the messages it flagged, how many were really scams. Low precision means the tool cries wolf and you stop trusting its alarms.

Recall — of the real scams, how many it caught. Low recall means dangerous messages slip through unnoticed.

False positive rate — of the legit messages, how many were wrongly accused. This is the metric people feel personally: accusing your friend's harmless Venmo text of being a scam burns more trust than one clever phishing mail surviving.

F1 — the balanced score of precision and recall combined, useful when neither direction of error is clearly worse.

Heuristics like these back up real spam filters, password managers' phishing warnings and browser Safe Browsing — no single signal decides, which is why adding independent evidence beats making any one check stricter again and again.
